## High-Value Product Differentiators

### 1. The "Tip" Engine (Premium+)
Standard booking systems often forget the "service" in service businesses. Our one-click tip selector before checkout is a favorite for salons and spas, often paying for the site itself in extra gratuities.

### 2. AI Business Concierge (Custom)
A custom-trained Claude widget that knows your business hours, prices, and services. It handles the "Are you open?" and "How much for X?" questions 24/7, reducing admin load by up to 40%.

### 3. Automated PWA Reach (Premium+)
No App Store friction. When users add the site to their home screen, they get native-style push notifications for booking reminders and promotional blasts.

### 4. Enterprise PDF Export (Custom)
Full HTML-to-PDF invoice generation triggered automatically via Resend after payment. Perfect for B2B clients and law/medical professionals.
