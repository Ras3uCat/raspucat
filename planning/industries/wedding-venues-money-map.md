# Money Map — Wedding Venues

## Problems Ranked (Boring Problems Framework + Recurring Revenue Filter)

### Problem 1 — Ghost Inquiries & Tour No-Shows
**Score: 10/10**
- Pain: Immediate, daily, measurable in lost revenue
- Expensive: 45–60% no-show rate × avg $10K booking value = $4,000–$15,000/month per venue
- Buildable: Automated reminder sequences + qualification forms — not AI hype, just reliable automation
- Recurring: Venue owners will pay monthly forever to solve this
- ROI justifiable in 5 minutes on a sales call

### Problem 2 — Directory Dependency (The Knot / WeddingWire)
**Score: 8/10**
- Pain: Chronic, expensive, owners are angry about it
- Expensive: $500–$2,000/month in wasted ad spend + coordinator time
- Buildable: Owned website + SEO + lead capture — well within our delivery capability
- Recurring: Monthly retainer for managed website + SEO

### Problem 3 — No Review Generation System
**Score: 7/10**
- Pain: Slow burn — venues don't realize how much it's hurting until they compare to competitors
- Expensive: Hard to quantify but 100-review gap = measurable inquiry volume difference
- Buildable: Simple post-wedding email trigger — 2-day build
- Recurring: Part of a platform play, not a standalone

### Problem 4 — Open Date / Shoulder Season Filling
**Score: 7/10**
- Pain: Visceral — empty Januaries feel like failure
- Expensive: 15–20 unfilled dates/year × $8K avg = $120K–$160K/year in lost revenue
- Buildable: Date promotion automation + warm list outreach — achievable
- Recurring: Ongoing managed service

### Problem 5 — Manual BEO & Contract Workflow
**Score: 5/10**
- Pain: Real but adjacent — Tripleseat and Planning Pod already address this
- Expensive: Coordinator time, but not existential
- Buildable: Yes, but head-on competition with established players
- Recurring: Possible but not differentiated

## The Pick

**Problem: Ghost Inquiries + Tour No-Shows**

This is the clearest, most painful, most immediately solvable problem in the wedding venue
industry. It's daily friction, not background noise. Every venue coordinator feels it every week.
The ROI calculation is simple enough to do on a napkin:

> "If you run 15 tours a month and 50% don't show, that's 7–8 wasted tour slots. At your 30%
> close rate, that's 2 bookings you never got. At $10K average — that's $20K/month in leakage.
> We charge $397."

The fix is not complex: a pre-qualification form that filters budget/date/guest count, and an
automated 5-touch reminder sequence that fires before every tour. Neither requires AI. Neither
requires complex integrations. Both can be built in 2 weeks and delivered as a managed website
+ booking system.

**The angle that unlocks it:** Don't pitch "venue CRM." Pitch "tour no-show solution." It's a
specific, measurable problem with a specific, measurable fix. Lead with the ROI math. Close with
a live demo of the reminder firing.
